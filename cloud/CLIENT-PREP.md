# Before your setup call: a 15-minute checklist

Your texts-in-Claude setup lives in **your own accounts**, so only you can
get to your texts. Doing these few things before the call makes the call
itself about 30 minutes shorter. You don't need to install anything.

## 1. Check your phone (2 minutes)

- It's an **Android phone**, and **Google Messages** is your texting app.
  (iPhones won't work.)
- Open Google Messages, tap your profile picture, then **Device pairing**.
  Note the **Google account** it shows. That's the account we'll use.
  If it says no account, that's fine; tell us on the call.

## 2. Make a Google Cloud account (5 minutes)

This is the small computer that keeps your texts. It costs about
**$3.65 a month**, billed to your card.

1. Go to **console.cloud.google.com** and sign in with your Google account
   (the one from step 1 is easiest).
2. Accept the terms when asked.
3. Click **Billing** (or "Activate" / "Start free" if it offers it) and add
   a credit card. If it offers free trial credit, accepting it is fine.
4. That's all. Don't create anything else; we'll do it together.

## 3. Make a Cloudflare account (3 minutes, free)

This is the secure connection between your texts and Claude.

1. Go to **dash.cloudflare.com/sign-up**.
2. Sign up with your email and a password, then click the link in the
   confirmation email.
3. If it asks what you want to do, skip or pick the **Free** plan. No card
   is needed.

## 4. Check Claude (1 minute)

- You can sign in at **claude.ai** in a web browser, on a paid plan
  (Pro or above) so you can add a connector.

## 5. For the call itself

- **Your phone**, charged, unlocked and nearby. At one point you'll tap a
  matching emoji on it.
- **Your passwords** for Google, Cloudflare and Claude, and your phone for
  any 2-step sign-in codes.
- About **45 minutes**. You'll sign in to a few things on the computer
  running the setup; you won't type any commands.

## What happens at the end

When everything works, the setup removes all of the helper's access to your
accounts and shows you a short report of what it removed. After that, only
you can get to your texts. The helper keeps two things, neither of which
shows any messages:

- **Status alerts** by email if your connection stops.
- **A status link** that shows only "connected or not" and how long ago your
  newest text arrived.

If you ever need help again, you let them back in for that session, and the
same hand-back runs at the end.
